# Coding-agent instructions — public repository and licensing boundary

These instructions apply to every coding/research agent operating in this **public** repository.
They are an operational publication/licensing boundary, not a change to any historical licence or
scientific result.

## 1. This repository is public

Treat every branch, commit, issue, pull request, workflow log, artifact, attachment and prompt copied
into this repository as potentially public disclosure.

Never place credentials, confidential third-party material, private task-bank material or selectively
private R&D here.

## 2. M086+ public research may continue here

Starting with M086, materially new core R&D is **public after pre-publication review by default**, not
private merely because it is new or commercially valuable.

Before the first enabling implementation/disclosure of a new core mechanism, read and apply:

- [`docs/IP_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md`](docs/IP_PUBLICATION_POLICY.md);
- [`IP_ASSET_REGISTER.md`](IP_ASSET_REGISTER.md);
- [`docs/DUAL_LICENSING_STRATEGY.md`](docs/DUAL_LICENSING_STRATEGY.md).

If the project owner's recorded disposition is `PUBLIC_AGPL_COMMERCIAL_OPTION` or another public
disposition, implementation, tests, protocols and results may proceed in this public repository under
the normal scientific rules.

Public project-controlled software is AGPL-licensed. Where the necessary rights are controlled, the
project owner may separately grant commercial permissions to customers under written agreements.
Do not confuse that commercial option with a prohibition on AGPL-compliant commercial use.

## 3. When agents must fail closed to a private workspace

Do **not** publish enabling material here when a specific recorded disposition requires temporary
confidentiality, including:

- `PATENT_FIRST`;
- `TRADE_SECRET_PRIVATE`;
- `CONFIDENTIAL_THIRD_PARTY`;
- `CONTRACTUAL_EMBARGO`;
- `COMMERCIAL_PRIVATE`;
- security-sensitive/credential material.

For those cases, stop before generating or committing the enabling details to this repository and use
the procedure in [`docs/PRIVATE_RND_OPERATIONS.md`](docs/PRIVATE_RND_OPERATIONS.md).

When publication status is genuinely ambiguous and no owner decision has been recorded, stop before
enabling disclosure and request/record the publication disposition. Do not guess that all M086+ work
is private, and do not guess that all new work is safe to publish.

## 4. M085 remains scientifically special

M085's independent external-maintainer requirement must remain genuine. Do not replace it with
project-authored material or evidence generated by a project-controlled AI agent merely because the
rest of the repository is public.

## 5. Scientific boundaries remain mandatory

Licensing/IP strategy must never be used to alter the scientific record. In particular:

- do not repair a frozen protocol after seeing a result and present it as precommitted;
- do not hide or rewrite a negative result;
- preserve Track A / Track B attribution boundaries;
- do not claim a generality/completion gate without the evidence required by the frozen criteria;
- keep independent evaluators independent of the component whose change they validate.

Do not merge externally authored copyrightable material merely under DCO sign-off while the current
contributor-rights boundary is active. Follow [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Preserving alternative commercial licensing requires a clean chain of rights for future external
contributions.
