# L9-OE1 v3 prospective qualification protocol

Status: **must be frozen before first v3 behavioral execution**.

V3 is a clean successor to the retained v1/v2 evidence. V2 produced a strongly positive operational result, but post-run review found that its scientific freeze omitted transitive executors and that its archive audit did not independently re-execute every retained evaluator receipt. Those findings are not patched retroactively. V3 uses a new, previously unconsumed population and freezes both the closed executable manifest and the stronger audit before any v3 behavior.

## Materialized observation programme

- Population seed: `86753243`.
- Exact population: 768 committed task records (192/domain).
- Domains: relational SQLite, regular expressions, structured JSON, binary compression.
- Horizon: 12 epochs/domain.
- Tasks: 16 tasks/epoch/domain.
- Acquisition epochs: 0–2.
- Transfer epochs: 3–11, growing from 2 through 10 learned three-slot motif blocks.
- Arms: coded-archive, archive-g7, greedy-g7, cold-g7.
- Maximum charged native executions/task: 14.
- Qualification native execution is isolated.

The campaign reads the committed population bytes. It does not regenerate the qualification population.

## Closed freeze

The freeze binds the exact population, v3 campaign, v3 audit, coded constructor, native evaluator/worker, G7 search engine and worker, generated-policy grammar, inherited guard/sandbox machinery, commitments helper, V36 engine/bank import, and the exact qualified G7 policy bytes.

Any bound-byte change invalidates the v3 certificate.

## Fail-closed execution and retention

Every stream must start in a newly created directory. Epoch zero refuses prior state. Every later state digest must match the preceding state. The terminal coded state is reloaded and its persisted codebook is independently regenerated before the stream can complete.

Every charged candidate call is retained with candidate genome, source hash, native outputs, evaluator receipt, construction metadata, solve flag, and negative flag.

The adjudicator requires the exact set of 16 arm/domain streams and the exact ordered task IDs/hashes from the frozen population. It recomputes task solved status, failure lists, receipt counts, negative counts, semantic discovery and rediscovery from candidate-level receipts rather than trusting summary booleans.

## Frozen acceptance predicates

V3 is operationally positive only if all are true:

1. all 16 frozen arm/domain streams are present exactly once;
2. every arm retains the exact ordered frozen tasks and task hashes;
3. every stream begins from the exact default state and every state/recovery link is exact;
4. no task exceeds 14 charged executions;
5. every charged candidate receipt is internally consistent and retained;
6. coded-archive solves at least 95% of transfer tasks in aggregate;
7. coded-archive solves at least 90% in every domain/transfer epoch and records at least four newly first-solving semantics there;
8. coded-archive beats archive-g7 by at least 15 percentage points aggregate and 5 points in every domain;
9. coded-archive beats greedy-g7 and cold-g7 by at least 20 percentage points aggregate;
10. coded-archive uses no more charged executions than archive-g7;
11. each coded domain retains four acquired motif parents and codebook descendants for blocks 2 through 10;
12. every coded transfer epoch/domain contains at least one rediscovery;
13. all task failures, zero-discovery windows and negative candidate evaluations remain explicit;
14. the terminal coded state/codebook verifies against its frozen constructor.

A post-run certificate is valid only if the **already-frozen v3 audit** also:
- verifies compressed/raw evidence hashes and the closed freeze;
- re-adjudicates all 16 streams;
- maps every record to the exact frozen task;
- independently re-executes every retained candidate against that task with the frozen native implementation and rebuilds its evaluator receipt;
- rejects any mismatch in native output, source identity, passed slots, score, task hash or receipt;
- re-verifies every terminal coded codebook.

A single false predicate or audit mismatch is a retained negative v3 result. Seed, population bytes, mechanisms, thresholds, budgets, arms, audit rules and source manifest may not be changed after freeze.

## Claim boundary

A positive audited v3 result may close the project-defined finite **operational L9 gate**. It remains project-authored empirical evidence, not a mathematical theorem of asymptotic open-endedness, general RSI or AGI. L10 remains open and requires separately governed independent/private or externally authored replication.
