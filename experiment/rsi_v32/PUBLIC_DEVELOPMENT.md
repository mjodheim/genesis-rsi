# V32 development uses consumed data

V31's 101, 211, 9143, 27189 and 40733 streams (240 tasks) are all consumed.
They are development data here, including V31's formerly fresh population.
No V32 fresh task has been executed.

Post hoc examination of V31's final archive recency proposals, using the old
external evaluator on those consumed tasks, found 84 retrieved champion proposals
per fresh seed. Respectively 40/84, 39/84 and 41/84 for seeds 9143, 27189 and
40733 scored below the current identity root. None was an exact solution. These
are diagnostic counterfactuals, not new prospective evidence or free actor calls.

Before the first V32 development sweep, declare every tolerance: 0, 25, 50, 75,
100, 150, 250 and 1000 quality-milli points. Rank successful programs by their
nearest historical successful-task identity quality to the currently observed
identity quality, then success recency, success count and source hash. G7's exact
acquired selector chooses retrieval when this distance is within tolerance and
cold generation otherwise. Only the revealed scalar quality and past receipts
enter that diagnostic; task names, target programs, inputs and future quality do
not. Scalar similarity can alias different tasks, so usefulness is empirical.

Select the tolerance lexicographically by total solved tasks, total best quality,
negative charged evaluations, then smaller tolerance, over all five consumed
streams. Preserve full episodes for all eight adaptive variants and all matched
controls. Development executes the actor locally for speed and carries no gate.
Canonical work must use isolated workers after a separately committed freeze.

Controls: exact G6 selector removal (always retrieves the closest two because
its absent selector defaults to identity); fixed recency retrieval of two; fixed
closest-one retrieval; fixed cold generation. All run unchanged G7 for the actual
search and charge one root probe plus one controller call plus up to twelve
candidate evaluations. No newly acquired G8, open-ended L9 or independent L10 is
claimed by this human-directed routing apparatus.
