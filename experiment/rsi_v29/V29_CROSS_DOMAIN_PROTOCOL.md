# V29 — unchanged L6 machinery on four native domains

Prospective L7 experiment, 1 October 2026. V28 established bounded L6 with
three automatic causal transitions. V27 remains an original preserved negative.
This experiment transfers the byte-exact V28 G6 policy to four new native
repair tasks. No V29 candidate-policy transfer has occurred.

Domains were listed in the original pre-development publication review:
relational SQL, regular expressions, structured JSON, and binary compression.
Final project-authored wrappers, oracles, all input generators, source variants,
and source-region order are fixed before the first transfer observation.
Their actual algorithms execute in independently maintained SQLite, CPython,
and zlib environments as described in `ENVIRONMENT_PROVENANCE.md`. There are
four algorithmic domains and three maintenance groups. These cases do not
supply independent task authorship or satisfy M085/L10.

Each task has three real executable loci with four declared variants, giving
64 distinct native source identities. Reference is (0,0,0), seed is (1,1,1).
Children change exactly one locus, in source-region order then numeric variant
order, without any unseen outcomes. The balanced 8/6/6 operation coverage is
fixed independently of policy stopping/scheduling thresholds. Preparation
executes reference and seed only and has no candidate-policy transfer.

The policy sees revealed quality and static changed-region/structure hashes.
Expected case answers are never passed to native proposal code. All oracle,
execution, budget, and evidence authority stays external. Policy decisions
remain in the unchanged isolated V27 pure ABI, and native implementations run
in a separate bounded process. External caps for every arm are 9 requests,
8 rounds, parallelism 2, and mutation depth 3. Preparation cost is common
apparatus overhead, not a credited search-policy saving. Reused evaluator
receipts still charge a represented request in every matched search.

Arms are exact G6, exact qualified G3, G4, G5, and every whole acquired-component
G6 ablation (persistence, ordering, scheduling). Every arm runs on all four
domains. Utility is solved-at-1000 count, quality, negative requests, negative
rounds. L7-positive requires a preserved qualified L6 lineage, unchanged G6
source identity, all four native domains executed, strict G6 improvement above
G3 in **each** domain, strict ordering-component ablation loss in **each**
domain, immutable complete task/evaluator/runtime commitments, equal budgets,
and one exclusive canonical attempt. No per-domain policy tuning is allowed.

This is a transfer ceiling, not an additional G6-to-G7 descent claim. The three
L6 transitions were already tested causally on their own fresh cumulative
population. This bank tests generalization of unchanged improvement machinery.
G4/G5 and memory/scheduler ablations are descriptive controls and any ties are
retained. The native bank may demonstrate ordering transfer while earlier
memory or scheduling mechanisms remain necessary on their own L6 populations.
No later reinterpretation can make a descriptive tie an ablation victory.

A failed conjunct is a negative; no domain, input, source, criterion, or arm
can be removed or repaired after observation. The full apparatus is committed
before a complete scientific freeze; that freeze is committed before the
exclusive marker. All original native outputs, receipt hashes, source lineages,
costs, rollback identities, and negative evidence are checkpointed. Verification
replays policy decisions against retained receipts and does not rerun native
proposal code. Anthony Mets directs the research; Codex assisted implementation.
