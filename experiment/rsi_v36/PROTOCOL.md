# V36 — learned native blocks under whole-body recombination

Prospective Track B protocol, 4 October 2026. Preserve every V35 input and result.
The question is whether successful state-owned fragments, with unchanged G7 and
the same fourteen-call cap, support discoveries when prior prefixes no longer
continue. This tests a limitation of V35; it does not redefine general L9.

## Environment and state

Reuse V35's actual SQLite, regex, JSON and zlib executable bodies, references and
isolated worker. Genomes remain finite operator vectors without a fixed length
catalogue. The first three epochs acquire four three-slot motifs through the same
single-slot append curriculum. Each motif starts with a distinct operator; its
remaining operators are prospectively seeded. After acquisition, each epoch has
four distinct target bodies, formed by independently drawing those motifs at
each aligned block position. Transfer epoch one has two blocks; every subsequent
epoch adds another block. Prior positions can all change. No nearest-parent,
Hamming-distance, observed-success or solvability filtering is allowed. Duplicate
target rejection within an epoch uses targets only, before any behavior.

Each of the four epoch targets occurs equally often in a seeded shuffled stream.
The actor receives slot count and scalar quality; task targets, target identities,
input contexts, references and per-slot correctness remain evaluator-owned.
Source/semantic identities mean canonical behavior within the witnessed native
DSL, not general program equivalence or new kernel semantics.

## Four matched arms

All arms retain complete evidence and use the byte-exact qualified G7 policy,
fourteen candidate executions per task, eight rounds, parallelism two and depth
three. One null-root call, at most four paid successful-body screens, at most
`12 - screens` search calls and one exact re-execution are charged.

- `archive`: retrieve up to four successful bodies and admit acquired block splices.
- `archive-point`: identical successful-body retrieval; single-slot children only.
- `greedy`: retrieve only the latest successful body and extract from it only.
- `cold`: no retrieved body or learned fragment; single-slot children only.

Retrieval uses genomes and past success only: longest/latest first, then repeatedly
choose the body with greatest minimum padded Hamming distance to selected bodies,
breaking ties by length, latest success and digest. Discard candidates longer than
the current task. No branch label or hidden target informs selection.

From admissible previously successful bodies, extract complete non-null aligned
three-slot blocks. Deduplicate by operator tuple; sort lexicographically and retain
at most thirty-two. Record the earliest admissible successful donor source. These
are acquired executable values; the extraction/splicing algorithm is host-provided.
At each parent, enumerate replacing each aligned complete block with each acquired
block, then all single-slot mutations. Deduplicate resulting sources and record
the first construction. G7 orders and chooses these proposals through its unchanged
ABI; no host ranking by private correctness is allowed. Record splice position,
fragment operators and successful donor ancestry for every evaluated proposal.

Keep every paid call, even unsuccessful probes and duplicates. Verify every native
output, policy decision, donor derivation, novel solve, rediscovery, budget and
ledger transaction in full replay. All costs include paid screening and confirmation.

## Population and stopping

Apparatus fixtures and the development pilot use only seeds 71 and 113, six epochs
and eight tasks per epoch, all four domains and arms: 1,536 tasks, at most 21,504
native evaluations. Development runs locally and are explicitly not fresh native
qualification. Commit the apparatus and protocol before the pilot. One declared
archive/SQL stop at epoch three after four completed tasks reconstructs state in
a fresh process; no paid prefix execution repeats.

Pilot progression requires, in every seed/domain, all warm-up archive tasks solved,
at least one first-solving semantic discovery in each of the three transfer epochs,
strict transfer coverage advantage over each control and no more calls than cold.
If any predicate fails, preserve the entire negative and do not spend V36 fresh
seeds. This is a feasibility screen, not a qualification threshold adjustment.

Only if that screen passes, commit a separate freeze before first fresh behavior:
seeds 940127, 1490233 and 2718281; ten epochs, sixteen tasks per epoch, all arms and
domains, 7,680 tasks with at most 107,520 evaluations. Fresh mode uses native and
policy workers, and requires the same predicates over all seven transfer epochs,
strictly growing solved/archive state, branching, rediscovery, full recovery and
replay. Report all zero-discovery windows, complete denominators and cost. Neither
finite positivity nor fragment reuse automatically closes general L9, G8 or L10.
