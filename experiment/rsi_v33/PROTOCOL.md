# V33 — prospective, paid active-memory comparison

This is Track B apparatus around byte-exact G7, not acquisition of G8. V31 and V32
negative evidence remains immutable. All their populations are consumed public
development here. The declared sweep selects only an adaptive gain margin.

## Externally fixed authority and costs

Every task pays one identity evaluation, exactly two distinct candidate probes,
one isolated controller invocation and at most ten search candidate evaluations:
maximum fourteen charges. Search uses unchanged G7, eight rounds, parallelism two,
depth three and the inherited pure-source/resource worker. The two probes are
fully observed current-task work, not free predictions. No input, target, task
name/family or future evaluation enters a policy/selector worker. All observed
sources, including failed probes, stay in the archive.

Rank prior successful sources of the same width by the V32 historical root-quality
distance, recency, success count and hash. Probe the first two; fill empty slots
with the first distinct identity neighbours. Choose the highest observed probe
quality, ties by probe order. G7 uses exploration when that probe improves on the
identity by more than the frozen margin, otherwise generation. Exploration starts
search at that probe; generation at identity. Every probe remains in final quality
and solution accounting even if it is not the selected search root. Previously
charged probe sources are excluded from subsequent search proposals.

Controls: exact G6 selector removal forces the best probe root; fixed recency
probes the two most recent successes; greedy probes one recent success plus a
local neighbour; cold probes two local neighbours and starts from the best if
strictly better than identity. All five arms pay the same calls and share search
caps. Fixed warm controllers may start from a probe worse than identity; their
observed identity result remains counted. These are explicitly different root
selection policies, not evidence that a new selector was acquired.

## Single fresh finite assay

Seeds 203999, 287107, 361373; eight windows; widths 3–10; twelve tasks per window;
288 tasks per arm. The generator and all task bytes are committed before first
behavior. Each seed/arm starts empty. Stop after six complete tasks and reconstruct
from disk, then finish. Reserve once, preserve all fifteen journals and complete
raw bytes before replay/adjudication. An unfinished task consumes its full cap;
no hidden retry, omitted failure, reseeding or replacement result is allowed.

The primary **finite archive utility** claim requires all of:

1. Complete frozen evidence, correct caps, replay, ancestry and disk recovery.
2. Adaptive solves strictly more tasks than cold and greedy.
3. Adaptive total cost is no more than cold; no per-seed solved regression vs cold.
4. Both identity and probe search roots are exercised.
5. Archive size grows and at least one genuinely new solving source is observed
   in every seed/window.

Report the stricter all-control solved advantage and exact selector ablation
separately regardless of their signs. This protocol tests usefulness of retaining
branches; it does not qualify the acquired selector merely by beating cold.
Selection of controls as successors after outcomes is forbidden.

## L9 and L10 boundaries

This bounded, one-domain comparison cannot pass open-ended L9. Report actual
discovery rates, including decline and exhaustion; never assert an asymptotic
positive rate from it. A positive result permits a prospectively frozen sustained
programme with continued task diversity and immutable external governance. A
negative result requires a new labelled successor, never edits to this assay.
L10 remains reserved for genuine external fork tasks, reproduction and audit.
