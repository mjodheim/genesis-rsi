per-seed cold regression on all five consumed seeds. The V48 engineering replay

four matched arms (adaptive, recency, greedy, cold), fourteen charged evaluations
per task: one identity, two probes, one controller, at most ten search requests.
Eight first-prefix epochs, seeds 104395301/104395337/104395373. No behavior on these
seeds before the input manifest and freeze are committed publicly. Task generation
shares the historical outcome-independent V35 curriculum; no target-based proposer

Adaptive retains four behavior-distinct compatible successful archive donors;
recency retains two recent donors, greedy one, cold none. Initial probes and

one-step donor descendants. All arms share grammar edits and inverse inference.
The proposer ranks hypothetical programs by disagreement between their predicted

G7 selects and orders this proposal population. Predictions are never evaluator

paid feedback are retained separately. No evaluator authority is acquired.

## Work and isolation

The target evaluator and G7 policy run candidates/decisions in isolated workers.
Proposal inference uses a bounded, project-authored grammar interpreter, which
receives only domain, inputs, candidate genotypes and paid scores. Every call and
length-weighted primitive visit is metered, capped identically in all arms at
8192 output calls and 67108864 primitive visits per task. Exceeding a cap interrupts
and consumes the attempt. Interpreter operations match generated source behavior;
they do not consult the real evaluator. Proposal work is reported separately from
the historical target-evaluator charge metric. Neither metric proves equal wall
clock or memory usage; no such efficiency claim is permitted.

## Evidence and decision rule

Use at most six independent seed/arm workers for execution and isolated replay.
Publish and externally commit an immutable reservation before each epoch.
An atomic STARTED marker consumes the attempt before its first behavior; a crash
never authorizes rerunning the reserved population. Retain all four arms, failures,
isolated search receipts, complete journals, recovery at task position three,
raw gzip bytes, independent deterministic adjudication and completion receipt.
Commit each complete epoch externally before continuation. Never replace or rerun
a reserved, interrupted, or completed epoch. Replay checks every evaluated program,
G7 decision, donor selection, inference meter and reported result.

For the first eight epochs, require: all frozen evidence complete; strictly
positive adaptive discovery and archive source/behavior growth on every seed in
every epoch, with branching; more total solves than cold and greedy; no more total
target-evaluator charges than cold; no per-seed cold solve regression. Report
proposal work independently, including ratios to controls. No result authorizes
silently changing grammar, seeds, windows, budgets or novelty definitions.

Novel discovery retains the original conservative criterion: solving behavior
minus every previously observed behavior, including previous failed candidates.
Only passing all predicates yields measured_continuing_archive_positive. L9 and
L10 stay false for this finite prefix. Continuing evidence beyond the first prefix
must keep the frozen apparatus and sequential external receipt chain until the
existing representation capacity (128 instructions); capacity is an explicit
limitation, not an unbounded archive claim.
