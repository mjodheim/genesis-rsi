## Fixed family and public development

G2's exact source is the identity. Candidate axes are strategy
(inherited/quality_first/frontier_mix), stopping quality (780/900/1000) and
stall rounds (3/5/8). A mutation changes exactly one axis to another declared
value. The complete universe within two mutations has 19 unique sources.
The frontier_mix component jointly sets internal parallelism to 2 and returns
the best eligible continuation plus the root when both can be explored; this
single declared component is ablated together. Other strategies keep G2's
parallelism 1. External request, round and parallelism authority is unchanged.

The public population is a complete Cartesian product: first quality 0/500/800,
winner root/first/second, width 2/4/6, winning continuation depth 1/2,
nonwinning continuation depth 1/3. There are 108 episodes. Low-quality decoy
continuations are included mechanically for every nonwinning branch, rather than
being authored selectively for a candidate. Public development caps are 9
requests, 8 rounds, at most 2 parallel parents and graph depth at most 4.

Eligibility tests the **composed** hypothesis: a successor must have an acquired
parent-selection component and stop at full quality 1000, strictly improve
development utility over G2, strictly lose utility after every acquired-component
ablation, and exhibit two differing nonempty parent choices on the same public
view against an exploration-component ablation retaining all other components.
This composition requirement is fixed before canonical arms; weaker numeric or
partial-stopping improvements cannot qualify this question.

The meta bridge is fixed uniformly: exact identity 750, unqualified proposal 650,
eligible composed causal successor 800. Open strategy branches first at G2;
after a mutation test stopping compositions first. Axis target values sort as
strings, so full-quality 1000 is tested before 900. No ordering reads unseen
outcomes. All sources, public receipts, ablations, witnesses and the entire new
native bank are committed and hashed before scientific selection.
