# V35 — domain-age curriculum and diverse paid memory probes

Prospective successor to the preserved negative V34 public pilot, declared before
V35 implementation and behavior. Two hypotheses are tested together, without a
claim that this identifies their individual causal contributions: introducing new
domains at local age zero avoids conflating domain transfer with target depth;
distinct observed behavior signatures avoid spending both memory probes on aliases.

Use the unchanged V34 grammar, witnesses, quality functions, G7 controller, caps,
four controls, cost accounting and strict novelty definition. Adaptive alone
deduplicates its ranked successful memory entries by their past witness signature
before selecting at most two. Recency and greedy retain their V34 definitions;
cold retains no program. Every retrieved candidate is reevaluated at full cost.
Witness equality is observational, not proof of semantic equivalence. No target,
input, domain age, task label or witness output is passed to G7.

All arms see the identical stream. Domain d first appears at global epoch d,
starting with one-operation targets. Its target depth is local age + 1 thereafter.
Each epoch includes four current branches plus branches 0 and 2 from the previous
local age. Input generation, branch construction and order are fixed before the
pilot; no filtering for solvability, novelty or favorable outcomes is permitted.
Fresh seeds: 15485863, 15485917, 15485927. Initial prefix: eight epochs, 468 tasks
per arm. Public development uses consumed seeds 503 and 887, four epochs, 120
tasks per arm, nonisolated execution; it never qualifies fresh performance.

Keep complete pilot data and each failure. Do not tune this implementation after
the pilot. A negative pilot is a valid stopping point; proceeding to fresh data
requires a separately committed freeze of all apparatus and predecessor evidence.
Fresh execution uses the inherited isolated workers, immutable reservation before
behavior, exact journal recovery at task three, and separately committed complete
epoch receipts. No pending reservation can be retried. The same archive persists
through all epochs; four different arms never share observations.

Qualification uses exactly V34's finite predicates: complete eight-epoch evidence;
branching and strictly growing source/behavior archives; at least one strictly new
solving behavior for every adaptive seed/epoch; more adaptive solves than both
cold and greedy, no per-seed cold regression, and cost no greater than cold.
Report recency separately. Do not promote a previously seen partial behavior to
novelty when it first solves. Zero discovery, capacity exhaustion, incomplete
evidence or governance mismatch suspends positive monitoring. Resource capacity
remains 128 instructions. Every negative epoch remains visible.

All work is project-directed Track B with zero scientific external model calls.
L9 open-endedness and L10 independence remain unpassed by any finite positive
prefix. Neither threshold relaxation nor relabeling this result is authorised.
