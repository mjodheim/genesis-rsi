This document supersedes the toy apparatus as the active V25 execution protocol.

predecessor is the byte-exact **G2 from positive L4 V22R**, not the failed V24 G3.

Can preserved G2 use its acquired bounded improvement process to discover a
causally useful parent-selection successor, retain the represented positive L4
histories without loss, and strictly outperform G2 and the G1-meta-selected
successor on a new, frozen population?

## Development and fixed search

The entire public population is the Cartesian product of first quality
0/500/800, winner root/first/second, width 2/4, and continuation depth 1/2:
36 graph episodes. Native transfer tasks do not enter this population.
The full bounded grammar contains 30 executable candidates within two mutations
of G2, including the exact original as the identity. All candidate sources,
development receipts, component ablations and available behavioral witnesses
are frozen before the canonical arms. This full public calibration is disclosed;
developers are not claimed to be blind to it.

The pre-freeze pilot showed that stopping-only numeric changes can win raw
development utility. Consequently the research objective is explicitly a
**causal exploration successor**. Eligibility uniformly requires strict
development utility over G2, an acquired strategy/depth/novelty component,
a nonempty differing parent choice against a matched acquired-exploration
component ablation on the *same* public revealed view, and
strict utility loss after removal of every acquired component.
Stopping-only empty/nonempty differences are insufficient.

The first witness-calibration pilot is preserved under development_history:
requiring two nonempty choices against exact G2 produced no eligible candidate
because its acquired early stop masks parent selection above 780. The final
prospective witness isolates the exploration component while retaining the
candidate's other components. Strict utility loss against that exact component
ablation remains mandatory. This calibration refinement occurred before any
canonical meta arm or candidate-policy transfer execution.

Meta evaluator quality is fixed at 750 for the identity, 650 for every
unqualified candidate, and 800 for a qualified causal improvement. This
eligibility-to-quality bridge is independent of the arm. At the identity,
strategy mutations precede novelty, depth, stopping and stall mutations; after
a mutation, stopping compositions precede strategy, novelty, depth and stall.
Numeric neighbors are adjacent values; strategy neighbors include all other
strategies. Target values then sort as strings and source digests break ties.
This prospective ordering tests exploration composed with stopping and never
sorts children by an unseen evaluation result.

The four canonical arms are A=g2_meta (exact G2), B=g1_meta (exact G1),
C=g2_ablation (only G2's acquired early-stop block removed), D=no_meta (fixed
FIFO, descriptive). Every arm has external caps of 9 represented requests,
8 rounds, parallelism at most 2, mutation depth at most 2. A policy may use less
than these caps. Its own frozen metadata can stop earlier. A request denotes
one externally evaluated executable proposal; development and evaluator
overhead are disclosed separately and are not an energy measurement.

Select only eligible *revealed* descendants, by lexicographic development
utility (solved count, summed quality, negative requests, negative rounds), then
fewest acquired components, then smallest source digest. If none is discovered,
retain exact G2 with a recorded no-qualified-successor outcome. Apply the same
rule to all arms. The generator follows each selected parent; record executable
bytes, parameter state, parent links and every redacted decision view.

## Retention and gates before transfer

Replay the represented V22R initial root decision and terminal quality-1000
decision for all three historical tasks using the immutable isolated sandbox.
All must preserve root acquisition and immediate stop, representing the positive
quality/request/round histories with zero loss. Bind the positive adjudication
and original transcripts. This is stronger policy-history replay than the old
terminal-only check, but is not a rerun of private native hosts or model calls.

Before any successor accesses the native transfer bank require:

1. Exact identities and committed full freeze; all 13 V24 negative records intact.
2. A qualified discovery through A's actual parent-selected lineage.
3. A development utility strictly above G2 and every component ablation.
4. The same-view parent-choice witness independently reproduced in isolation.
5. Zero-loss L4 represented-history retention.
6. A's meta result strictly above C under (qualified discoveries, selected
   development utility's first two terms, negative meta requests, negative rounds).
7. A new native bank and validated reference/seed instruments frozen before
   canonical A/B/C/D selection.

Failure preserves a negative pre-transfer verdict; it does not consume the bank
with candidate policies. An instrument failure is a preserved aborted attempt.
Never silently restart a canonical attempt or modify a freeze after observation.

## New native population and final positive rule

Four task identities are fixed in native_transfer.py: Cellar batch stock/shipping,
Cellar order-line total/name snapshot, Arcade Fruit tail occupancy/path blocking,
and Arcade Goose car collision/honk cooldown. Each uses a byte-exact public host
snapshot, two declared production-expression faults, and native Java or Node
execution. Independent external harness files implement fixed semantic cases.
The candidate generator cannot edit or read the harness, cases, budget or evidence
ledger; the policy sees only already-revealed aggregate outcomes/actions.
Child order is source digest, independent of outcome. Reference and seeded root
are calibrated before freezing; no successor is run on transfer during development.
No V24 task or production target file is a new V25 target.

After all pre-gates pass, run A's selected executable G3, exact G2, B's selected
successor, and every single-component G3 ablation exactly once on all four tasks
under the same external caps. Repeated identity arms remain separately recorded.
The final utility is (tasks at 1000, sum of best quality, negative represented
requests, negative rounds), compared lexicographically without reweighting.

V25 L5-positive requires strict fresh global utility over **both** exact G2 and
B's selected successor, strict fresh loss for every acquired-component ablation,
all pre-gates, exact evaluator/source receipts, equal external caps, genuine
parent-selected descent, no fresh-bank selection or leakage, and all immutable
negative evidence preserved. Any failed conjunct is a valid negative result.
Only a positive adjudication satisfying every predicate can support the bounded
V25 L5 claim. It does not retroactively turn V24 positive.

## One-shot execution and preservation

The full scientific freeze binds the committed apparatus, policy/sandbox sources,
the complete calibration, native sources/harnesses/tasks, retention references,
V24 preserved evidence and this protocol. A subsequent commit records that freeze.
The canonical runner verifies its ancestry and every byte before atomically
creating an attempt marker. All intermediate results and failures are preserved.
Canonical reruns are refused. Any successor experiment needs a new protocol and,
after a consumed transfer result, a genuinely new bank.
