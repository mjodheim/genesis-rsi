# V30 — prospective bounded L8 bottleneck selection

Anthony Mets directs the research; Codex assists implementation. The predecessor
is the exact V28 G6 policy, with V29 native L7 transfer. No prior frozen input,
negative, global IP register or permanent CI workflow changes.

## Hypothesis and scope

The inherited G6 improvement machinery can discover a pure measured-bottleneck
selector. Its most recent scheduling acquisition must causally reduce the cost
of that discovery. The selected G7 must choose which part of its own improvement
pipeline to repair from measured evidence, exceeding every matched fixed-target
control on a new population. This is a finite fault-injection assay of disposable
pipeline copies, not unconstrained tooling or open-ended self-modification.

Three parts correspond to actual inherited mechanisms: exploration combines
archive pressure and stagnation handling; generation is proposal ordering;
scheduling trims redundant root work. Each context disables exactly one inherited
gene. The sole repair operator restores the chosen gene to its qualified G6
value; choosing an intact part is a no-op. This common fixed operator and the
fault injector are external apparatus, not powers owned by a candidate.

## Evidence boundary and development

The selector sees only a fixed diagnostic dictionary and the four allowed target
strings. It never receives the fault label, context/task identity, graph, oracle,
unrevealed outcomes, source files, credentials or network access. Diagnostics
use revealed baseline evaluations and static proposal regions: positive-quality
plateaus, skipped new regions, and concurrent redundant root work. Their
conditioning on already revealed success/failure is part of the hypothesis.
The baseline probe is evaluated and charged equally to every arm.

`PUBLIC_CONTEXTS.json` is an explicitly outcome-conditioned public curriculum,
not a holdout. The complete 324 examined public contexts and all fixed-repair
traces, including negatives, are retained losslessly in
`PUBLIC_CONTEXTS_ALL.json.gz`. Twenty examples (16 exploration, 3 generation,
1 scheduling) are selected by declared digest order and diagnostic eligibility.
No fresh context is selected or filtered by these conditions. The fresh vectors
were authored before the selector implementation and first selector behavior.

Eight executable family members append three enabled-counter flags to the exact
G6 bytes. Zero flags are the exact parent, whose target ABI defaults to identity.
Each mutation enables one term. Proposal descriptors are static AST differences.
Public quality is the fraction of actual chosen repairs that strictly improve
the baseline and equal the best fixed repair. Qualification requires all twenty
improvements and loss when each enabled term is removed. The inherited policy,
not a human, selects parents, ranks proposals and discovers the qualified member.
The full family is prospectively committed; no candidate is edited between the
canonical discovery and transfer.

## Canonical single attempt and equal budgets

A complete apparatus commit and a separate committed freeze must precede any
fresh-policy observation. Freeze identity covers all prior scientific inputs and
results, source universe, diagnostic code, curriculum including discarded cases,
fresh vectors, protocol, guards, tests, predecessor and caps. An exclusive marker
is created before any canonical arm; replacement or retry is forbidden. Every
exception preserves an instrument abort. A failed pre-gate remains a negative
without consuming the fresh population. All observed evidence remains retained.

The meta search and matched G6 scheduling ablation each have 12 requests,
8 rounds, parallelism 2 and depth 3. The lexicographic meta utility is qualified
discovery, best quality, negative represented requests, negative rounds. A tie
in selected source is allowed; a strict efficiency gain is required.

The fresh population has 58 unfiltered contexts: 16 exploration, 24 generation,
18 scheduling. Exact vectors are in `fresh_bank.py`; they are disjoint from every
development/consumed graph vector. Each probe and each post-repair search has
9 requests, 8 rounds, parallelism 2 and depth 4. Every arm receives one isolated
target decision and one repair opportunity. All probe/post requests and rounds
plus one target call are charged, including identity and wrong-part choices.
Uniform receipt memoization only reduces physical duplicated executions; it does
not reduce represented cost. No external scientific model calls occur (Track A).

The eight arms are adaptive G7, exact parent G6 (identity), all three fixed
targets, and all three selector-term ablations. Utility is lexicographic solved
post-repair contexts, summed post quality, negative total requests, negative total
rounds. Adaptive G7 must strictly exceed the parent and **every** fixed target,
including the best in hindsight, and each selector-term ablation on the complete
population. It must actually choose all three parts at least once. Individual
ties, identity choices, missed diagnostic signals and nonoptimal repairs stay in
the record; they are not grounds for excluding a task.

## Retention, adjudication and rollback

The G7 core must remain the byte-exact qualified G6 prefix. Old native V29 G6
receipts are replayed with G7 through the same isolated search ABI to establish
retention of four full-quality domains. This is labelled consumed receipt
retention; it is not fresh native evaluation or extra domain generality.

`POSITIVE_BOUNDED_L8` requires every executable predicate in `campaign.py`.
Failure yields `VALID_NEGATIVE_TARGET_SELECTION`; an exception yields
`PRESERVED_INSTRUMENT_ABORT`. The checker independently replays public
calibration, isolated meta choices, old native receipts, every fresh baseline,
diagnostic, isolated target decision and post-repair search using only retained
receipts. It rejects filtering, altered budgets, unequal probes, substituted
sources, unobserved requests and forged evaluator receipts.

The permanent exclusive attempt index binds a deterministic lossless gzip raw
record by byte and content digests. All fresh rows and controls remain complete.
Selected G7 bytes and exact parent digest provide rollback. Scientific success
does not authorize a candidate to alter evaluator rules, deployment policy,
repository permissions, credentials or merge rules. Task authorship remains
project-controlled; this is not M085/L10 independence or an AGI completion claim.
