# OE1 native prospective assay

This successor is frozen after the consumed V36 development diagnosis and before any
behavior on the unused V36 fresh seeds.

## Purpose

The experiment asks whether a target-blind acquired-constructor policy can preserve
positive discovery across four materially different native software domains while
composition depth and archive diversity grow.

It is a strong scoped L9 precursor, not by itself a proof of asymptotic open-endedness.
The authoritative L9 criterion remains `docs/RSI_GENERAL_SUCCESSOR_MAP_2026-09-23.md`.

## Frozen population

The population reuses the already committed but never executed V36 fresh seeds:

- 940127
- 1490233
- 2718281

Domains:

- relational-sql
- regular-expressions
- structured-json
- binary-compression

Each seed/domain stream contains ten epochs and sixteen tasks per epoch. Epochs 0-2
acquire four three-slot motifs. Epochs 3-9 require non-prefix whole-body recombination,
reaching 24 slots/eight motif blocks.

No task identity, seed, expected output or target sequence is available to the policy.
The diagnostic policy receives only previously acquired successful motifs and the scalar
`matched_slots` value after a paid native execution.

## Frozen arms

1. **diagnostic** — OE1 scalar acquisition + hypothesis-separating constructor.
2. **archive** — preserved V36 diverse archive with learned splices.
3. **greedy** — preserved V36 latest-successful-body control.
4. **cold** — preserved V36 no-memory control.

All arms use the same native evaluator and maximum fourteen charged executions per task.
Every exact solution is independently re-executed.

## Diagnostic mechanism

During acquisition, each unknown slot is probed with operators 1, 2 and 3. If none
improves the scalar matched-slot count, operator 4 follows by elimination from the
externally fixed native alphabet.

After four three-slot motifs have been acquired, a target body is represented as an
unknown sequence of motif identities. The actor maintains all compositions consistent
with the transcript. At each paid step it deterministically proposes a motif composition
whose predicted scalar outcomes partition the remaining hypotheses as evenly as possible.
Candidate query generation is a deterministic function only of block count, search step,
remaining-hypothesis count and the acquired motif matrix. Fresh task IDs/seeds never
participate. A unique posterior is executed and independently confirmed.

## Frozen acceptance predicates

The scoped assay passes only if every predicate is true:

1. all committed tasks for every arm are retained;
2. no arm exceeds fourteen charged evaluations on any task;
3. the diagnostic arm solves every committed task;
4. diagnostic acquisition epochs 0-2 are complete in every seed/domain;
5. every diagnostic transfer epoch 3-9 produces at least one newly first-solving semantic;
6. diagnostic transfer solve count strictly exceeds archive, greedy and cold for every
   seed/domain cohort;
7. diagnostic total charged evaluations are no greater than cold;
8. diagnostic archive size and solved-semantic size strictly increase every epoch;
9. diagnostic archive has four branches after acquisition and records rediscovery;
10. exact replay/verification reproduces every diagnostic receipt.

No threshold may change after the first fresh behavior. Negative windows and failed
predicates are retained.

## Claim boundary

A positive result establishes a prospective sustained multi-domain native
acquisition/recombination assay under fixed external governance. It does **not** alone
establish mathematical asymptotic open-endedness or independent L10 replication.
