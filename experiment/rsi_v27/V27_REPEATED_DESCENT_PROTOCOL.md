# V27 — three repeated improvement transitions

Prospective protocol, 1 October 2026. V26 is the qualified bounded L5 parent.
There has been no V27 canonical attempt or candidate access to the new bank.

This asks whether exact G3 can select G4, which selects G5, which selects G6,
without a human choosing the winning mechanism between transitions. The claim
is bounded to finite, project-authored represented search tasks. It is not a
claim of native fresh transfer, independent task authorship, M085, L7, L8, or AGI.

## Fixed family and public selection

The exact G3 source SHA256 is specified in `family.py`. Four semantic genes
have values 0/1/2: plateau ranking, candidate ordering, evaluation scheduling,
and persistence with archive-aware ranking and structural deduplication.
Persistence stall allowances are 3/7/8 rounds. Scheduling thresholds are
1000/900/750. External authority stays immutable. The public matrix contains
81 semantic programs. Four comment-only layouts give 324 source identities,
explicitly not 324 different behaviors. Comments and positions do not add
capabilities. AST structure and changed AST leaves are outcome-independent
proposal descriptions; their hashes never encode evaluation results.

One mutation changes one adjacent gene value or one adjacent layout value.
Acquired genes are locked in subsequent generations. Each transition is typed
as acquisition of exactly one new semantic component; proposals with two new
components are syntactically excluded before evaluation. Layout neighbors
precede semantic neighbors; semantic region order is persistence, plateau,
scheduling, ordering. Every arm has the same generator and source population.

Selection uses the lossless PILOT_005 public calibration: the 108 historical
V26 development graphs plus complete public partitions of 96, 48, and 144
graphs. The active partition is released by the fixed stage counter, not chosen
by a human or by a holdout result. The bridge assigns 1000 to qualified
one-component descendants and ranks others at 500..999 by active-partition
utility. Qualification requires strict improvement on the current partition
and cumulative utility, no loss of previously solved episodes, and strict
cumulative loss after each acquired-component ablation. Selection considers
only actually revealed candidates, then cumulative utility, fewest gene values,
smallest layout, and source hash. A controller halts at quality 1000.

Each source is executed in an isolated process under the unchanged V23 source
guard and a pure V27 ABI. Decisions see revealed qualities and static proposal
descriptions only. External meta caps are 12 requests, 8 rounds, parallelism 2,
and mutation depth 3. Costs include neutral proposals. No model calls occur.

The bootstrap matched controller is exact qualified G2, removing the composed
G3 acquisition while retaining the same G3-rooted candidate family. At later
stages the causal comparator removes the most recently acquired component
from the controlling parent. All older single-component ablations are also
reported, including ties. Older components need not remain indispensable for
every later discovery; they must remain causal on cumulative required tasks.
The sixth public pilot shows that older persistence is redundant in discovering
G6. This fact is retained, not relabelled as an ablation win.

## Retention, new tasks, and positive rule

Canonical public selection completes all three transitions before any new task
is evaluated. Each descendant must solve all four consumed native V26 tasks
at 1000 using the original Java/Node evaluators. These are retention, never
fresh evidence. Public and previous fresh solved episodes may not regress.

The new graph bank is fixed in `fresh_bank.py`, with 12 tasks per partition.
Each is a previously unevaluated parameter vector, disjoint from the public
development bank. Finite axes are selected before the apparatus freeze, with
no adjustment after observing a candidate on this bank. Every generation is
compared with its parent and every acquired-component ablation on the full
cumulative new population. Caps are 9 requests, 8 rounds, parallelism 2, depth 4.
Utility is lexicographic: full-quality solved count, summed quality, negative
requests, negative rounds. A cost-only gain is permitted and must be labelled
as such. No tolerance or weights may be changed after execution.

L6-positive requires all three public discoveries, bootstrap and both subsequent
strict causal meta advantages, all native retention checks, strict fresh utility
gain at each transition, strict loss after every acquired-component fresh
ablation, zero loss of previously solved fresh tasks, exact source lineage,
preserved freezes and ancestry, and one canonical attempt. A failed conjunct
is negative. A negative does not authorize tuning on this bank or a retry.

The full apparatus is committed before its scientific freeze is built. The
freeze is committed before an exclusive attempt marker is opened. Each meta
phase, retention episode, and unique source/task transfer episode is checkpointed.
Repeated source/task combinations reuse a retained receipt; represented costs
remain charged within each matched episode. Verification replays decisions
against retained receipts and never queries a fresh evaluator.

All six development pilots remain archived under their original commits. The
first five fail to demonstrate the chain. Anthony Mets is the sole human
research director; OpenAI Codex assisted design, implementation, and validation.
