## Fixed coordinate scheduler

For interfaces of at most three slots, all arms use point construction to avoid
interference during initial motif acquisition. At every slot, first evaluate a
body with that slot zero, then each operator one through four on the same base.
Choose the paid body with greatest total matched count, breaking ties by source
digest. Include the previously measured current body among choices.

For larger interfaces with admitted fragments, traverse aligned blocks. First
pay to zero the whole block, then try each available learned fragment on that
base, with at most thirty-two extracted values and deterministic lexicographic
order. Choose by the same scalar rule. A gain of exactly three over the zeroed
block certifies completion of that block within the witnessed DSL. Otherwise use
the identical point constructor for its three slots. Bodies with no fragments use
point construction directly. Stop on an exact whole-body solve, always confirming
it in one further native execution. Record the scalar choice groups, donor sources,
changed positions and all retained/rejected paid proposals.

The scheduler is a trusted project-authored host interface. Fresh executable
bodies run in isolated native workers; no separate isolated G7-policy claim is
made, and G7 policy-call count is zero. Replay verifies the host's deterministic
choices as well as all actual output/reference receipts. It is internal replay,
not independent replication.

## Continuation model and scope

If all four exact motifs survive, zeroing a block exposes its outside contribution;
trying its four learned values yields a unique maximum with all three slots right.
Block coordinates are independent. Thus arbitrary recombinations at any number
of blocks are constructible in at most five probes per block plus screening and
confirmation, under `5 * slots + 6`. Point control needs up to five probes per
slot. This argument is conditional on a complete acquired library, independent
coordinates, exact scalar counts, distinguishable kernels and sufficient native
runtime; it is not arbitrary environmental openness or primitive invention.

Calls scale linearly with block count. With four new target bodies per eight-task
epoch, the worst-case discovery/call lower bound is `4 / (8 * (15*b + 6))`, tending
to zero as `b` grows. Retain this limitation and measured rates explicitly. A
positive scoped construction assay does not close general sustained L9, new G8,
L10 independence or RSI/AGI completion. No frozen gate is weakened.
