# Original negative observation and explicit evidence recovery

The original V25 canonical run completed under the committed full scientific
freeze and printed a valid negative: G3 improved aggregate utility over G2 and
the G1-meta successor, but the matched strategy ablation was identical to G3.
The native bank was consumed. L5 was not validated.

Before the local raw attempt could be committed, the execution workspace reset
to its earlier snapshot. The apparatus and freeze had already been committed
remotely and remain exact. The original final terminal JSON is preserved in
V25_ORIGINAL_OBSERVED_OUTPUT.json. Unavailable original raw traces must not be
silently invented or attributed to another run.

This recovery protocol and the original observed output are committed before
one explicitly named diagnostic replication. It executes only the original
frozen policies, generator and native evaluators on the already consumed bank.
It makes no new fresh-transfer claim, no changed selection, and no positive L5
claim. Its complete adjudication must match every observed original field.
Any divergence is preserved as a recovery failure, never a repaired result.

V25_RECOVERY_REPLICATION.json holds the new replication traces and its actual
runtime. V25_ATTEMPT.json is then an evidence envelope referring to the original
run, explicitly marking its raw traces as reconstructed by replication and the
original raw traces as unavailable. Canonical attempts remain one; the separate
recovery replication count is one. V25_FINAL_ADJUDICATION.json preserves the
observed original negative. Existing evidence replay checks verify the retained
trace semantics; the provenance tests enforce the recovery disclosure.

This is weaker archival provenance than preserving the original raw record
directly, even if all deterministic observations reproduce. It cannot be used
to claim a fresh bank, an undisclosed second canonical attempt or positive L5.
Any subsequent L5 experiment requires a separately frozen new population.
