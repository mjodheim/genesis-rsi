# V34 — corrected probe accounting, negative adaptive development

**L9 remains open.** This is a complete consumed-data DEVELOPMENT comparison,
not fresh qualification, an acquired G8 or an independent result. The adaptive
candidate does not beat its controls. The original V33 accounting defect and all
original artifacts remain preserved separately.

V34 retains every paid probe candidate, including failures, and counts any actual
exact solution from probes or search. Duplicate actual calls retain their full
cost; each source appears once in the persistent program archive. Probe-only
descendants retain identity-root ancestry. Those retained receipts can change
later retrieval; this is an actual corrective rerun on consumed tasks, not a
post-hoc arithmetic replacement of V33's original report.

## Complete development comparison

All 456 consumed V31/V32 tasks ran in all four arms, eight seed streams each.
Thresholds, population order, controller identities and external caps were
unchanged from V33: three probes, one controller call, at most ten search
evaluations, eight rounds, parallelism two, depth three, fourteen calls per task.
Every actual search remains byte-exact G7. The fast comparison is non-isolated;
actual isolated policy/candidate execution and recovery were tested separately.

| Arm | All observed solved / 456 | Charged evaluations | New solving sources | Below-root / evaluated search retrievals |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Three-probe adaptive G7 | 291 | 4,732 | 68 | 44 / 162 |
| Scalar adaptive G7 | 312 | 4,689 | 76 | 160 / 371 |
| Exact selector removal; always nearest two | **331** | **4,381** | **80** | 264 / 701 |
| Cold | 287 | 5,079 | 76 | 0 / 0 |

The predeclared descriptive adaptive criterion fails because adaptive does not
solve more tasks than every control. It costs no more than cold and loses no
coverage against cold in any stream. Against scalar there are two adaptive-only
and twenty-three scalar-only solves; against always-nearest-two there are fourteen
adaptive-only and fifty-four control-only solves. All these negatives are retained.

The fixed memory control solves 44 more tasks than cold with 698 fewer charged
evaluations (13.7%). This is a useful finite development observation: retaining
all probe outcomes makes an archive control substantially stronger here. It is
not a post-outcome promotion of that control to a qualified adaptive successor.
Lower below-root retrieval frequency alone was an inadequate utility target;
partial programs and branches can contribute despite a poor immediate score.

This remains one bounded transducer domain. New solving **source** counts do not
equal newly first-solving semantics or new improvement machinery. They cannot
establish an asymptotic rate, arbitrary task diversity or L9 open-endedness.
