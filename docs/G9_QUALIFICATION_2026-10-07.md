Every untouched self-model component is inherited by digest. N+1 therefore names N as its exact parent and increments generation by one.

The holdout contains three balanced blocks:

- **4 parent-retention cases**: fresh direct C# subscript tasks already reachable by N;
- **4 G6-component-gain cases**: fresh C# method-return subscript tasks where the old universal matcher cannot emit but the G6 descendant can;

## What this establishes

Under the project's bounded software-repair setting, Genesis N generated a materially changed Genesis N+1 from accumulated qualified experience, before seeing the fresh evaluation set. N+1 then outperformed N from **4/12 to 12/12** under the same candidate budget, preserved every parent success, and lost the expected gains under two independent causal ablations.

This is evidence of **one bounded causal self-improvement transition**.

## What this does not establish

- One N -> N+1 transition is **not recursive self-improvement**.
- G10 remains open until descendants themselves repeatedly contribute to producing better descendants on fresh evidence.
- The task families and evaluator are project-created, not independently governed third-party validation.
- The result is software-domain and mechanism-bounded; it is not general RSI, AGI or ASI.
