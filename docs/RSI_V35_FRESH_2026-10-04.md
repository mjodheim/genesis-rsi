## What this establishes about L9

The archive is useful, diverse, growing and recoverable in a prospective native
extension environment. A separate [conditional induction](V35_APPEND_CONTINUATION_ARGUMENT.md)
explains how the next extension remains constructible under the same charged-call
cap for arbitrary mathematical sequence length, if its stated assumptions hold.
This avoids extrapolating an asymptotic claim solely from twelve positive epochs.

The assumptions remain substantive: four host-provided native operator families,
append-only target changes, surviving correct branch frontiers, discriminating
references and workers that complete. There is no newly acquired constructor or
improvement policy. The archive fails an actual development counterexample that
rewrites many old slots, despite retaining four branches. Large-body CPU and
memory eventually hit real worker limits. The conditional model and measured
assay therefore do **not** establish general open-ended Genesis discovery.
