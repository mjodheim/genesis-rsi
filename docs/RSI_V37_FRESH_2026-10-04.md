## What changed after V36

[V36's negative](RSI_V36_RECOMBINATION_2026-10-04.md) remains byte-exact: learned
splices helped but acquisition gaps, root choice and fixed depth prevented
continuation. V37 separates initial point acquisition from reuse. Its new trusted
coordinate scheduler zeros a block, pays to try acquired donor values and selects
the greatest scalar matched count. It receives no hidden targets, expected answers
or per-slot correctness in that selection. Every donor is a previously successful
executable body; removing block proposals yields the matched point-control cost.

This scheduler is project-authored apparatus and **replaces G7 search scheduling**.
There are zero G7 policy calls in V37, not a demonstration that G7 discovered the
new constructor. There are zero external scientific model calls. Native bodies
execute in isolated Python workers; scalar planning runs in the trusted host.
Replay re-executes known bodies locally against original references and rebuilds
choices; it is internal audit, not fresh evaluation or independent replication.

[The conditional argument](V37_PAID_CONSTRUCTION_ARGUMENT.md) covers arbitrarily
many rewritten motif positions under complete library retention, independent
coordinates, distinguishing witnesses, exact scalar counts and sufficient worker
resources. It removes whole-body prefix dependence. The cost is at most five
probes per block plus screening/confirmation, and therefore grows with length.
The conservative discovery/call lower bound tends to zero. Fixed three-slot motifs,
four host-provided primitives per domain and internally authored task composition
remain. Primitive invention, endogenous planner acquisition and general sustained
L9 are not established by this finite positive or its conditional model.
