# Toward L9 — prospective sustained programme draft

Prepared on 4 October 2026 under the owner's instruction to continue toward L9.
This is a proposal for further work, not an accepted qualification protocol or
evidence of a gate. The authoritative criterion remains
[`RSI_GENERAL_SUCCESSOR_MAP_2026-09-23.md`](RSI_GENERAL_SUCCESSOR_MAP_2026-09-23.md).
L9 is still open; L10's separate independence requirement remains unchanged.

## What is already established, and what is missing

V31/V32 preserve branching descendants, negative candidates, task-boundary
recovery and rediscovery under an external immutable evaluator and budget. Their
rates decline and their domain is a finite width-2–64 rotation/XOR grammar. V32's
adaptive memory comparison is a retained negative. Neither more widths nor more
seeds removes that structural ceiling.

V33 tests a specific memory diagnostic on consumed data, with three paid probes
and four matched controls. It can diagnose retrieval interference; it cannot
establish open-ended discovery, another acquired policy generation or a
cross-domain descendant process.

V33's original accounting omitted some successful probes and their archive
entries; that defect and its evidence remain preserved. V34 corrects it in a
separate consumed-data successor. Always retrieving the nearest two solves
331/456 against cold 287/456 with 13.7% fewer evaluations, while the adaptive
three-probe arm solves 291/456. This is a useful finite archive-control baseline
for engineering, not a qualified new recursive acquisition. Future measurement
must retain and count all paid candidates, including diagnostic probes.

The existing map does not specify an L9 observation duration, numerical rate
threshold, task-diversity schedule, stopping rule or acceptance authority. These
must precede qualification measurements. A finite result cannot establish a
positive asymptotic rate by itself, even if every measured window is positive.

## Proposed next implementation boundary

1. Introduce a sustained epoch driver, independent of any one bank. Before the
   epoch starts, publish a single-assignment manifest binding all adapter sources,
   evaluator identities, task order, policies, predecessor archive heads, caps
   and stop rules. Make retrospective repair or silent task replacement fail.
2. Separate task solutions from improvement-machinery descendants. Carry an
   executable policy archive with causal provenance; a new solution source is
   not by itself a new improvement capability. New lineage-owned constructors
   must be admitted through the existing trust-root/evaluation boundary.
3. Replace the global finite transducer catalogue with extensible bounded
   compositions. Each task retains a fixed external execution budget, while
   subsequent epochs may use already acquired constructors and deeper bodies.
   Commit the complexity schedule before observing results. Expanding a host
   hand-authored grammar is apparatus growth, not endogenous acquisition.
4. Provide materially distinct native adapters, starting from the repository's
   existing SQLite, regex, structured-JSON and compression execution kernels.
   Do not relabel XOR/rotation/affine widths as separate domains. Reusing consumed
   V29 repair cases is calibration only; future task construction and custody
   must be committed prospectively. The old three-locus grammar remains frozen.
5. Match branching archive, latest-only/greedy and cold controls on controller,
   diagnostic, candidate, failed, replay/adoption and verification costs. Carry
   all negative descendants forward. Recover at committed task boundaries and
   quarantine pending calls with their full reserved cost.
6. Make evaluator and ledger authority external to the actor. Actors receive
   revealed evidence and authorized descriptors only, with no expected answers,
   future task identity, permission changes, credentials or deployment access.

## Measurement record to freeze before qualification

For every arm, epoch and actual domain, retain:

- cumulative evaluated archive size, branching and executable parent identities;
- newly **first-solving** semantic behaviors, separately from never-observed
  source hashes and rediscovery of previously partial or solved programs;
- held-out behavioral descriptors and their exact measurement costs; descriptor
  differences are an operational diversity estimate, not global inequivalence;
- new improvement machinery and causally dependent later discoveries, with
  equal-budget predecessor/ablation comparisons;
- all coverage, regressions, retention and charged evaluations;
- discovery per charged evaluation, both absolute and over growing task/body
  complexity, with zeros and failed windows retained;
- uninterrupted versus fresh-process recovery, independent ledger head checks
  and rollback receipts.

Do not use an aggregate average to hide exhausted domains. Report per-domain
and per-window trajectories, cold comparisons and uncertainty. A positive
finite window is evidence about that window only. No automatic `L9_PASSED`
flag should be wired to the current development runner.

## Decisions still needed before a qualification run

The research director must establish an observation horizon and compute budget,
the prospective domain/complexity schedule, the novelty and retention utility,
and the external stopping/acceptance rule. A suggested engineering starting point
is resumable epochs of bounded tasks on all four native domains with archive,
greedy and cold controls, followed by fresh prospective tasks. This is not a
proposed shortcut from a fixed number of epochs to open-endedness.

Only after those choices are recorded should a successor apparatus and its
qualification population be committed and frozen. The current environment can
develop and test this machinery; it cannot substitute an AI's acceptance decision
for the missing scientific protocol or promise a positive research outcome.

A concrete initial **development** schedule to review is twelve epochs, thirty-two
tasks per epoch in each of the four native domains and archive/greedy/cold arms.
At fourteen charged evaluations per task this reserves 64,512 calls, plus any
separately declared verification/adoption budget. Commit all task/complexity rules
before this programme's first behavior. Observe every domain and epoch, including
zero discovery windows, with fresh-process recovery at epoch boundaries. This
bounded schedule can test the sustained driver and establish a finite baseline;
it neither supplies an L9 acceptance threshold nor proves an asymptotic rate.
