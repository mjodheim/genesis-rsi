# V49 continuation and native-work boundary

Post-pilot mathematical analysis, 5 October 2026. This does not change the frozen
protocol, success predicates or measured outcomes.

If the four preceding target pipelines have all succeeded and survived, their
whole source bodies are admitted construction operators. Each next target is the
serial composition of two of those four programs. Exhausting sixteen products
therefore includes the exact target, without coordinate separability, intermediate
truth or predictive simulation. Identity, at most four paid screens and exact
confirmation give a bound of **22 charged candidate calls**, within the equal
26-call governance. Newly successful programs supply the next tool library. This
is conditional constructibility; incomplete retention and native instrument limits
remain failure modes.

This argument establishes no unbounded novelty theorem. It guarantees only that
the target source is among the proposals. The measured diagnostic fingerprint may
already have been observed, including as a failed earlier proposal. More generally,
four finite SHA-256 outputs have finitely many possible fingerprints. Indefinite
strict diagnostic novelty is impossible in that finite space; no collision-free
infinite assumption may be smuggled into the conclusion.

There is also a native-work obstruction within the admitted sequential DSL. A
length-n body executes n real kernel steps on each of eight task/diagnostic
contexts. Every successfully solved task receives one original exact execution and
one paid confirmation. Complete coverage of eight tasks therefore costs at least
`8 * 2 * 8 * n = 128n` primitive visits per epoch, before failed proposals and
other screens. There are at most four newly solved target fingerprints, so:

`conservative novel discoveries / primitive visits <= 4/(128n) = 1/(32n)`.

Length doubles each epoch. Even an ideal search policy in this exact execution
model cannot keep a nonzero asymptotic lower bound per native primitive visit.
The actual pilot shows the decline. A positive candidate-call rate consequently
does not establish a positive native-work floor. This is an obstruction for V49's
chosen representation/execution contract, not a general impossibility theorem for
archive learning, program optimization or RSI.

The explicit next research problem is therefore not another longer copy of this
finite prefix. It is discovery in a richer task/representation space with a
work-sensitive continuation model, where useful acquired abstractions can change
later search or execution beyond substituting long sequential body values. The
fixed kernel alphabet, closed host curriculum and unchanged scheduler must remain
visible in that successor's controls. General L9 and independent L10 stay open.
