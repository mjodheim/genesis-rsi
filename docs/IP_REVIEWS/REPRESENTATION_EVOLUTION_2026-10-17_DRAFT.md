## Asset and novelty boundary

Scope: three exploratory modules under
experiment/representation_discovery/ (prototype, adaptive_basis, macro_search),
three test files, README, and deterministic nonqualifying development outputs.
The mechanism assembles reversible GF(2) axis/butterfly passes and arbitrary
coordinate shears into alternative representations of Boolean truth tables,
then uses bounded candidate search to select an encoding.

The primitive alphabet is **human-authored**. The system does not invent
arbitrary new execution semantics, does not evolve a novel interpreter,
does not improve its successor's future improvement rate, and does not
demonstrate general RSI. The full-Mobius transformation recovered is a
known mathematical operation, NOT a claimed new invention.

## Results, negative evidence and licence treatment

Three frozen-quality claims are explicitly **not** made: new general
representations, unseen-class transfer, and recursive gain of the mechanism
that makes improvements. The development trials use previously visible
families; optimization is an encoding-bit proxy, not elapsed time or energy.
The guided and equal-scored-candidate random baselines select the same
six-input solution, and the guided method spends more prescreening work.
Reported improvement compared to identity cannot beat the known
full-Mobius baseline after descriptor cost.

## A scientifically useful next experiment (not frozen)

A genuinely prospective successor must measure whether inherited
representational operations let the next improver discover NEW operators
more efficiently. Freeze an externally judged task-family/class split
and metric in advance. Compare (a) fixed representation/fixed search,
(b) search changes with fixed representation, (c) evolved representations,
(d) randomized equally metered mutations, and (e) known algebraic
transforms. Count full compute including prescreening and descriptor cost.
Require improvements that generalize to independently withheld classes,
causal ablation, and a second generation which increases the ability to
discover useful new representations. Keep independent validation outside
the optimizer and separate from any project-authored blind-bank simulation.

This is planning, not a preregistration or a result. It requires a new
explicit freeze and a truly independent evaluator before any qualifying run.
