# Diagnosis by intervention — 10 October 2026

Anthony directs that Genesis diagnose its own failures and name the limiting part of its
machinery before changing it. The observational attribution of this date named the fault
localizer; the paired trial DEV_COVERAGE_REPAIR1 did not bear that out. Under the standing
PUBLIC_AGPL_COMMERCIAL_OPTION decision, publish a bounded mechanism that names a component
only from interventions: the system is rerun on cases it failed, once unchanged and once
per variant, each variant changing one component; a component is named when its variant
recovers cases the unchanged rerun does not, under an exact paired test. A companion reader
sorts recorded repair attempts by how they ended and by the resources they left unused.

Review precedes the first run. AI development assistance is as recorded in
`docs/AI_ASSISTED_DEVELOPMENT_PROVENANCE.md`; model calls keep their provenance in the
trial records. No third-party code is copied. Ablation, paired designs and the sign test
are known techniques; no novelty is asserted for them. Software AGPL-3.0-only; prose
follows LICENSE_POLICY.md. No patent-first, trade-secret, confidential-third-party or
embargo reason identified.

No frozen experiment changes: sealed records are read, not rewritten. The repair loop and
the proposer gain two options that default to their former behaviour. Cases are
development cases the agent already failed; one arm reads the developers' edit sites and
is marked as such. No held-out case of the repair bench and no reserve case of the
external catalogue is involved. No general RSI claim follows from any outcome of this work.
