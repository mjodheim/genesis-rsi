# Genesis G10 strengthened recursive-successor protocol — 7 October 2026

## Status

**APPARATUS / DEVELOPMENT ONLY. No G10 qualification observation exists at this point.**

G9 established one bounded causal whole-successor transition. G10 asks the
stronger question: does inherited improvement machinery make a descendant
measurably better at producing the evidence and mechanism for its own
successor, repeatedly?

The campaign is deliberately stricter than “run self-editing several times.”

## Required chain

The bounded campaign must produce a content-addressed lineage:

`G0 -> G1 -> G2 -> G3`

Here G0 is the already-qualified G9 successor used as the G10 campaign seed.
Each transition must be frozen before its own fresh qualification holdout is
revealed.

Every child must:

1. contain a material machinery change produced from its parent's evaluated
   discovery evidence;
2. strictly outperform its parent on a fresh holdout under the same candidate
   budget;
3. preserve every fresh success of its parent;
4. use zero external-model calls in generation and runtime;
5. remain unable to own its evaluator, promotion rule or final verdict.

## Recursive-production criterion

A repeated improvement chain alone is insufficient.

For transitions 2 and 3, the current generation and its direct ancestor are run
on the **same discovery bank under the same candidate budget**. The current
generation must:

- solve more discovery cases than the ancestor;
- produce sufficient passing evidence to induce the next successor while the
  ancestor cannot;
- use fewer candidate executions than the ancestor in the frozen assay.

This is the central G10 causal test. It asks whether the inherited machinery is
useful not merely for solving tasks, but for **producing the next improvement**.

## Bounded capability ladder used by the assay

The assay exercises software-repair composition rather than adding a larger
statistical model.

- **G0 — retained single-site machinery.** Seeded from the qualified G9 system,
  including the qualified local G8 specialist.
- **G1 — paired retained-template composition.** Repeated successful evidence
  from two independent retained repair families is compiled into a two-edit
  candidate mechanism.
- **G2 — scope-bound pair search.** Passing composed repairs across distinct
  scopes support acquisition of scope-aware candidate search, reducing wasted
  cross-scope combinations.
- **G3 — iterated scope composition.** Repeated successful scope-bound repairs
  support one candidate that applies the retained pair across every matching
  scope.

Every level retains the lower-level routes as fallbacks. A child therefore has
to extend, rather than replace, its parent's fresh capability.

## Prospective chronology

Each stage uses the same sequence:

1. run a visible discovery bank against the current generation;
2. for stages 2 and 3, run the same discovery bank against the direct ancestor;
3. generate and content-address the proposed successor from discovery evidence;
4. commit/freeze that successor before the qualification holdout exists;
5. generate the fresh holdout outside the repository;
6. commit only the holdout SHA-256 and frozen apparatus identities;
7. reveal the holdout;
8. evaluate parent and successor under the same six-candidate-per-case ceiling;
9. preserve the result whether positive or negative;
10. advance only if the frozen stage gate passes.

A failed stage terminates the scientific chain. It may motivate later
development, but the failed frozen attempt is never repaired into a pass.

## Fresh holdout structure

Stage 1 contains six cases: two retained single-site cases and four fresh
two-edit composition cases.

Stage 2 contains eight cases: two single-site retention cases, two paired
retention cases and four fresh scope-binding cases.

Stage 3 contains ten cases: two single-site retention cases, two paired
retention cases, two scope-bound retention cases and four fresh multi-scope
iteration cases.

All raw cases must fail before repair.

## Resource boundary

All compared profiles receive the same maximum of **6 candidate executions per
case**. External model calls are **0**. Candidate execution count, controller
CPU process time and wall time are recorded.

The goal is not “more intelligence by more compute.” The recursive advantage
must appear inside the matched resource ceiling.

## G10 final gate

The final adjudicator may return `G10_SCIENTIFIC_GATE_PASSED` only when:

- all three prospectively frozen stage gates pass;
- the profile chain is digest-contiguous;
- campaign generations are exactly 0, 1, 2 and 3;
- every child strictly improves on fresh evidence;
- every child preserves every parent success;
- transitions 2 and 3 demonstrate recursive-production advantage over the
  direct ancestor;
- that advantage improves both discovery yield and candidate-execution cost;
- generation/runtime external-model calls remain zero;
- mutable Genesis never owns evaluation or promotion authority.

## Claim boundary

A pass would establish a **bounded, project-defined recursive
self-improvement chain in this software-repair composition assay**.

It would be reasonable evidence for domain-bounded RSI under this explicit
criterion. It would **not** establish general RSI, AGI, ASI, open-world
autonomy, or independent third-party validation. Those require separate
evidence beyond G10.
