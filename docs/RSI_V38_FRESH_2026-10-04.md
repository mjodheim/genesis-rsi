## Recursive acquisition and diversity

At level zero the actor acquires four one-operation executable bodies. At each
later level it constructs four distinct concatenations of two previous bodies,
selected by the externally frozen task stream. Each newly successful full program
is eligible as a whole construction macro for the following level. Its genotype
is copied into descendants and rendered as actual executable native source; record
successful donor source identity, acquisition position and parent ancestry.

The ancestor-only arm removes those newest macro values while retaining identical
whole-archive screening; it constructs with macros from one older level. This
repeats the intervention across increasing tool sizes, not merely the initial
reuse of a fixed primitive fragment library. All 2,024 evaluated archive splice
proposals have retained successful donor derivation. The outer coordinate
scheduler remains host-authored, replaces G7 scheduling and has zero G7 calls.
Acquired tool values are not a newly lineage-selected policy algorithm.

## Continuation argument and remaining L9 limit

[The prospective symbolic argument](V38_RECURSIVE_MACRO_ARGUMENT.md) shows that,
with all four newest exact macros retained and independent witnessed cells, the
two-coordinate construction needs at most sixteen calls including screens and
confirmation. Its own successful bodies then supply the next level's macros.
The shared twenty-six-call cap yields conservative discovery/call bound `1/52`
at arbitrary symbolic depth. This removes V37's `1/body-length` candidate-call
decline under the specified recursive composition model.

It does **not** remove native resource growth. Source, actual kernel operations,
outputs and storage grow exponentially with depth, so the unchanged worker limits
eventually constrain physical continuation. A constant candidate-call bound is
not a constant wall-clock bound. The two-program curriculum, independent slot DSL,
four admitted primitive kernels and fixed host scheduler remain. Arbitrary
environmental change, genuinely open task/algorithm invention, endogenous policy
acquisition and general sustained L9 are still unproved. No old gate is weakened.
